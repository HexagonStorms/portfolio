Campaign Overview

Boss Loot Adventures Vol. 03: A Trail into the Dark & The Hunger Beneath

Welcome to our ongoing D&D 5e campaign featuring two interconnected dark fantasy adventures. This campaign offers a thrilling journey into the depths of mystery and horror, perfect for adventurers seeking challenging encounters and immersive storytelling.

These standalone adventures are designed for 4 players at levels 6-7, but can be played as a continuous two-part story. The adventures are adaptable to various subterranean environments, making them perfect for integration into any existing campaign.

Getting Started

How to Join

  1. Join our Discord: First, join our campaign Discord server where we coordinate sessions and discuss the adventure.
  2. Create your character: Head to D&D Beyond to create your character (Level 6-7).
